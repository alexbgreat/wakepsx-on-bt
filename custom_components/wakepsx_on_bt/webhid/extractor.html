Wake PSX on Bluetooth

Read the paired console and controller MAC addresses straight from your DualShock/DualSense controller over WebHID — no need to plug it into the Home Assistant server itself.

  1. Plug the controller into this device (the computer/phone running the browser) via USB.
  2. Click "Connect Controller" below and pick it from the browser's device picker.
  3. Copy the two MAC addresses into the "Enter manually" step of the Home Assistant setup wizard.
Requires Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium-based browser, over HTTPS (or a local file).
Wake PSX on Bluetooth · runs entirely in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.
